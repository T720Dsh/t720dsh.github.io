About Me

I am a research master’s student in Data Science and Information Technology (DSIT) at Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School, Tsinghua University. My undergraduate background is in Information and Computing Science at Fudan University.

My current interests lie at the intersection of 3D/4D computer vision, animation, and AI agents. I am exploring how visual observations can be translated into editable 3D motion, and how agents can use reusable skills and visual feedback to create and refine animation.

My previous work includes reinforcement learning experiments for reasoning and interpretable modeling of medical images. These experiences shape my interest in combining mathematical modeling with practical, verifiable AI systems.

Reinforcement Learning for Reasoning

Studied reinforcement learning approaches to language-model reasoning, including group-relative policy optimization and KL regularization. Conducted controlled simulation experiments comparing a GRPO-lite approach with REINFORCE in a discrete question-answering setting.

Reinforcement learningGRPOControlled experiments
Scope of the experiments

The empirical comparison uses a controlled simulation environment. It does not establish improvements from fine-tuning a full-scale language model.
